# Acceptance cases

## Representative request
Infer a and b from observations y(t)=ab exp(-t).

## Required behavior
Identify only the product ab absent extra information; do not report unique a and b from a successful optimizer.

## General checks
- Verify the exact domain and hypotheses.
- Separate proved statements from observations.
- Report inconclusive outcomes honestly.
- Do not invent source citations or executed checks.
