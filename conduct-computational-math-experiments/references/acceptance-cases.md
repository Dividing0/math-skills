# Acceptance cases

## Representative request
Test whether all graphs with a given degree condition are Hamiltonian.

## Required behavior
State the finite graph range and generation completeness; validate witnesses; do not infer a universal theorem from the enumerated cases.

## General checks
- Verify the exact domain and hypotheses.
- Separate proved statements from observations.
- Report inconclusive outcomes honestly.
- Do not invent source citations or executed checks.
