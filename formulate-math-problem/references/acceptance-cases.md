# Acceptance cases

## Representative request
Formalize “find the fastest route” when travel times depend on departure time.

## Required behavior
Represent a time-dependent graph; state departure time, admissible waiting and the objective. Do not silently assume static nonnegative weights.

## Cross-cutting checks
- Preserve quantifier order and the stated domain.
- Label missing evidence and unresolved obligations.
- Do not claim proof, novelty or formal verification from tool-free assertion or finite samples.
