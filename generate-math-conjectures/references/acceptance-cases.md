# Acceptance cases

## Representative request
Conjecture a property of integer polynomials after testing the first 100 inputs.

## Required behavior
Report the tested interval and arithmetic, seek structural obstructions, and retain conjecture status.

## Cross-cutting checks
- Preserve quantifier order and the stated domain.
- Label missing evidence and unresolved obligations.
- Do not claim proof, novelty or formal verification from tool-free assertion or finite samples.
