# From patterns to falsifiable conjectures

## Choose a branch
- Integer or combinatorial patterns: write the domain and parameter bounds, use exact observations, and test extremal or degenerate families before extrapolation. A formula for a finite sample is initially an interpolation candidate.
- Structural conjectures: identify a preserved operation or invariant, test its behavior under products, quotients and limiting constructions when these belong to the class. A conjecture must specify whether constants are universal or object-dependent.
- Analytic patterns: distinguish pointwise, uniform, almost-sure and mean statements. State the norm or probability space before choosing numerical observables; one plot cannot decide a quantified convergence mode.

## Worked progression
For the first few odd numbers, sums are 1,4,9,16. A precise candidate is: for every integer n>=1, sum from k=1 to n of (2k-1)=n². Check n=1, then derive S_(n+1)=S_n+2n+1. If S_n=n², this equals (n+1)². The induction closes, so this candidate should be promoted to a proved identity and removed from the unresolved conjecture list. Evidence status changes only after the proof obligation is discharged.

## Tempting inference and counterexample
A long matching prefix cannot identify an unrestricted formula. Let a_n=n² for n<=100 and a_n=n²+1 for n>=101. The first hundred values coincide exactly with squares but the universal square formula fails. For a polynomial interpolation setting, n²+product from k=1 to 100 of (n-k) produces the same prefix and differs afterward. Extra structural assumptions, such as an independently proved degree bound, are needed to turn sample agreement into a theorem.

## Stop or hand off
If novelty matters, hand the exact claim and terminology variants to literature search; do not infer novelty from a missing search result. If a counterexample appears, preserve the original failed statement before proposing a narrower one, and explain the mathematical reason for its new hypothesis. Deliver the quantified conjecture, evidence provenance, failed versions, adversarial checks, and a discriminating next task. Keep candidate proof mechanisms separate from completed arguments and record whether computations actually ran.
