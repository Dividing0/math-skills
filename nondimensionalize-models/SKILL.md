# Безрозмірне формулювання моделей

## Workflow

1. Record units of variables, parameters and equations.
2. Choose scales from the physical regime and justify their nonzero values.
3. Transform every term and initial or boundary condition.
4. Identify independent dimensionless parameters and dominant regimes.
5. Distinguish a scaling choice from an asymptotically justified term omission.
