# Алгебраїчна теорія чисел

## Workflow

1. Specify the number field, embeddings, ring of integers and order being used.
2. Distinguish factorization of elements from factorization of ideals.
3. Verify integrality, ramification and discriminant computations with exact arithmetic.
4. Track class groups, units and local completions with the exact hypotheses of cited theorems.
