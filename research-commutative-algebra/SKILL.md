# Research Commutative Algebra

## Workflow

1. Specify the ring, module and Noetherian or finiteness assumptions.
2. Track ideals under localization, quotient and extension or contraction.
3. Check exactness, flatness and finite-generation requirements before using standard results.
4. Use dimension, associated primes or integral dependence with exact hypotheses; inspect counterexamples when assumptions are removed.
