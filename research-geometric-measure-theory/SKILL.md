# Геометрична теорія міри

## Workflow

1. Specify ambient dimension, measure normalization and geometric object class.
2. Distinguish Hausdorff dimension, rectifiability and finite perimeter.
3. Track almost-everywhere statements and exceptional sets.
4. Verify hypotheses for area, coarea and compactness results.
5. Separate classical boundaries from measure-theoretic and reduced boundaries.
