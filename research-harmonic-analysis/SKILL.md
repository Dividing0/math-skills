# Research Harmonic Analysis

## Workflow

1. Declare transform conventions, domain, measure and normalization.
2. Choose function or distribution spaces and justify convergence and inversion mode.
3. Check integrability before exchanging integrals and convolution operations.
4. Distinguish continuous transforms from sampled discrete transforms; track aliasing and window assumptions.
