# Гомологічна алгебра

## Workflow

1. Specify the ambient abelian category or module category, grading and sign conventions.
2. Verify differentials square to zero and maps commute with differentials.
3. Distinguish exactness, chain homotopy and quasi-isomorphism.
4. Check existence and suitability of projective or injective resolutions and boundedness assumptions.
5. Track functor variance and derived-functor indexing when interpreting Ext and Tor.
