# Research Information Theory

## Workflow

1. Specify random variables, channel model, dependence and logarithm base.
2. Distinguish discrete entropy and differential entropy; track support and regularity.
3. Check coding assumptions, block length and asymptotic regimes.
4. Separate achievable constructions from converse bounds and measured compression from theoretical limits.
