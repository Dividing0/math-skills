# Large Deviations

## Workflow

1. Specify random family, topology and speed.
2. Define candidate rate function and exponential tightness assumptions.
3. Check upper and lower bounds on the correct classes of sets.
4. Distinguish asymptotic logarithmic estimates from finite-sample probability bounds.

## Evidence discipline
