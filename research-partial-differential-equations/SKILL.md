# Research Partial Differential Equations

## Workflow

1. Specify domain, coefficients, initial and boundary conditions and compatibility.
2. Choose classical, weak or distributional solutions and relevant function spaces.
3. Check existence, uniqueness and stability with energy or other justified estimates.
4. Preserve boundary and conservation properties in discretization; distinguish discretization evidence from analytic regularity.
