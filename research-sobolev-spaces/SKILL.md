# Sobolev Spaces

## Workflow

1. Specify the domain, dimension, exponent, differentiability order and boundary regularity.
2. Distinguish equivalence classes from pointwise representatives.
3. Check embedding and trace hypotheses including critical exponents.
4. Use weak derivatives and distributional identities consistently.
5. Separate approximation, compact embedding and continuous embedding; track boundary conditions in closures.
