# Acceptance cases

## Representative request
Review a proof that swaps a limit and an integral because pointwise convergence holds.

## Required behavior
Locate the interchange, check a suitable theorem and its hypotheses, and report a gap or counterexample if unjustified.

## Cross-cutting checks
- Preserve quantifier order and the stated domain.
- Label missing evidence and unresolved obligations.
- Do not claim proof, novelty or formal verification from tool-free assertion or finite samples.
