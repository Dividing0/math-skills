# Research, proof and evaluation methods

Use this family-level guide together with the domain-specific workflow and boundary case. It supplies candidate strategies, not a theorem whose hypotheses may be skipped.

| Method | Choose when | Obligations | If obligations fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exact statement and dependency audit | Any theorem or research contribution is proposed | Normalize domains, quantifiers and source hypotheses | Preserve unresolved obligations rather than fill them with an invented lemma |
| Constructive proof or falsification | Need establish or challenge a claim | Try valid boundary witnesses and discharge every proof step | Separate a failed proof from a false theorem |
| Primary-source comparison | Need prior-work or novelty assessment | Read the decisive source and compare exact statements | A failed search does not establish novelty |
| Independent evaluation | Need evidence a workflow or skill helps | Use hidden fresh tasks, matched budgets and verified rubrics | Bundled acceptance cases are not held-out evidence |

## Task-specific anchor

Read [acceptance-cases.md](acceptance-cases.md). State which strategy above handles that case, what exact assumption is missing, and what can be concluded after repairing it. Do not assume the repair automatically proves the desired result.
