# Acceptance cases

## Representative request
Assess whether a proposed weaker assumption for a convergence theorem is novel.

## Required behavior
Compare exact hypotheses and proof dependencies; state a bounded novelty assessment and missing sources rather than assert priority.

## Cross-cutting checks
- Preserve quantifier order and the stated domain.
- Label missing evidence and unresolved obligations.
- Do not claim proof, novelty or formal verification from tool-free assertion or finite samples.
