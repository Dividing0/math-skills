# Domain playbook: survey-math-literature

Read this when selecting a technique, checking a decisive hypothesis, or preparing a mathematical conclusion. The family-level method guide is an orientation aid; the explicit gates below govern this skill. Examples are training material and must not be used as independent evaluation evidence.

## Method branches and applicability

1. For a focused theorem search, expand terminology and equivalent formulations, then inspect primary theorem statements. Record author, title, version, stable identifier and exact theorem or page locator; separate retrieved full text from metadata and secondary descriptions.
2. For dependency tracing, follow each decisive cited lemma to its source and compare definitions and hypotheses. A source might state a stronger regularity condition or use a different norm; preserve those differences in a claim-to-source ledger rather than importing the headline.
3. For novelty assessment, compare a proposed result against the accessible corpus along domain, assumptions, conclusion and constants. Use forward and backward citation searches where tools permit. Report search date, queries and unresolved coverage; zero search hits establishes only that this search found no match.

## Worked derivation

In a supplied fictional two-paper corpus, Paper A states that continuous functions on compact K attain extrema; Paper B states that Lipschitz functions on compact K attain extrema. Lipschitz implies continuity since |f(x)−f(y)|≤L d(x,y) tends to zero as y→x. Thus B's conclusion follows from A under B's hypotheses. The ledger records this internal redundancy; it does not establish historical priority or anything about papers outside the supplied corpus.

## Invalid inference and witness

A proposed “novel” theorem obtained by changing “bounded interval” to “compact interval” may simply rename the same closed bounded real interval condition. Conversely, an open bounded interval is not compact: 1/x on (0,1) is unbounded. Keyword matches and missing matches cannot resolve exact theorem equivalence.

## Stop and handoff

Return verified statements and precise locators, paraphrased or unavailable claims, search coverage and comparison arrows. If full texts or browsing are unavailable, perform only the supplied-corpus comparison and mark external novelty unresolved; never fabricate papers or priority.
